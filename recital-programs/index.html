UMKC Conservatory Recital Program Generator

Create a polished digital program for your recital — formatted to Conservatory standards, approved by your faculty, and published to the Conservatory’s online event calendar for your audience to open on their phones. No printing, no folding.

How it works — start to finish
  1. Build your program. Enter your recital details and repertoire, plus optional program notes and a performer bio. Chicago-style formatting is automatic.
  2. Send it to faculty for review. Download the Faculty Proof (a Word .doc) and email it to your applied professor. They read it and mark comments — they don’t edit the file.
  3. Make their changes here. Come back to this tool, make the edits your professor marked, and re-save your project file. Always edit here — never in Word.
  4. Send it to staff to publish. Once your program is approved, save your project file and send it to the Dean’s Office staff. They publish it to the Conservatory’s online event calendar, where your audience opens it on their phones.
💾 Saving & continuing your work
  • Method 1 — Automatically, in this browser. Step away and come back on the same computer and browser, and you can pick up right where you left off.
  • Method 2 — As a project file you download. Switching computers, want a backup, or ready to hand your approved program to staff? Save your project file — then reload it here anytime, on any machine, to keep working. This same file is what you send to the Dean’s Office staff to publish.

The file you send to your professor is different — that’s the Faculty Proof (a Word .doc), and it’s for their comments only. You don’t reload it here.
