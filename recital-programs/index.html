UMKC Conservatory Recital Program Generator

Enter your recital details and repertoire. The tool applies Chicago-style formatting — title treatment, catalog numbers, composer dates, and movement listings — and produces a modern web program your audience opens right on their phones. No printing, no folding — just a clean, shareable link to the program.

How it works
  1. Enter recital details — plus optional program notes and a performer bio
  2. Format each work using the guided wizard
  3. Download your web program (.html) — ready to share for review and display at the recital
Why a web program?
  • 🌙 Opens in dark mode — easy on the eyes in a darkened hall, with a one-tap switch to light
  • 📖 Expandable program notes & bios — tucked behind a tap, so the repertoire stays clean and scannable
  • 🔗 Tappable links — give to the Conservatory or browse upcoming events, right from the program
  • 📱 Looks great on any phone — single-column, no pinch-to-zoom, reads the same online or in the hall
💾 How your work is saved
  • Automatically, in this browser. Step away and come back on the same computer and browser, and you can pick up right where you left off.
  • As a file you download. Switching computers, want a backup, or sending it for faculty review? Download your program file — then reload it here anytime, on any machine, to continue and make changes.
