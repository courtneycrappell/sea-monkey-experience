SeeMe

A private, two-device tracing aid for artists

What it is

SeeMe turns two iPhones or iPads into a precision tracing setup. One device is mounted as a camera aimed at your canvas; the other stays in your hand, showing the live view with your reference image aligned on top — so you trace on the real surface, without a projector or laggy video.

SeeMe requires two iPhone and/or iPad devices on the same Wi-Fi network.

Quick setup

  1. Install SeeMe on both devices and connect them to the same Wi-Fi.
  2. On the mounted device, choose Camera, aim it at your canvas, and lock focus. A 4-digit pairing code appears.
  3. On the handheld device, choose Drawing Device, pick the camera from the list, and enter the code.
  4. Import a reference image, set its opacity, and line it up with move, scale, rotate, or the 4-corner perspective tool.
  5. Trace on the real canvas while watching the aligned overlay.

Troubleshooting

Camera not found? Confirm both devices are on the same Wi-Fi and that Local Network access is allowed for SeeMe in Settings on both devices.

Connection dropped? SeeMe reconnects on its own for about five minutes. If the camera restarted, it will show a new pairing code — choose it again and enter the current code.

Video paused? Another app took the camera, or the device paused it (Split View, heat). The message on the camera screen says what happened; video resumes automatically.
