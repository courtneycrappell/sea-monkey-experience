SeeMe Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

SeeMe does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. Camera video and reference images stay on your devices. SeeMe uses your local network only to send the live camera view from your camera device to your drawing device during an active session. SeeMe has no account system, advertising, analytics, or cloud service.

What SeeMe accesses, and why

  • Camera — used only on the device you assign the Camera role, only to show and stream the live view of your canvas to your paired drawing device.
  • Local network — used only so your two devices can find each other and stream directly, device to device, over your own Wi-Fi. The connection is encrypted.
  • Photos — you pick a reference image with the system photo picker; SeeMe receives only the image you choose, and it never leaves the drawing device.

What SeeMe does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or contact information.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
  • No cloud storage, uploads, or internet transmission of your video or images.
  • No sale or sharing of any data — SeeMe has none to share.
